Editorial principles

Independence, respect and clarity

Beauty Leaders is guided by editorial principles intended to protect trust, dignity and meaningful public dialogue.

Independence

Editorial choices should not be confused with commercial activity, sponsorship or product promotion.

Respect

Guests are approached as professionals and as human beings. Beauty Leaders avoids sensational or demeaning framing.

Clarity

The platform does not claim partnerships, endorsements or achievements that have not been formally established.

Conflicts of interest

Where relevant, the founder’s professional activities in the medical aesthetics industry are disclosed so that viewers and readers can distinguish between editorial work and commercial roles.

Medical information

Beauty Leaders is a media initiative, not a clinical provider. Content is intended for dialogue and education and does not replace medical evaluation, diagnosis or treatment advice.

Corrections and updates

If factual corrections are needed, they may be communicated through the contact route listed on the collaboration page.
