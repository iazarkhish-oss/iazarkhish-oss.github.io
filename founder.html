Beauty Leaders founder

Iman Azarkhish is the founder and host of Beauty Leaders. He has over 15 years of professional and international experience in the medical aesthetics industry, with work spanning medical technology, regulatory processes, market development, professional education and public dialogue.

He is also the founder and CEO of Shafa Tajhiz Azarakhsh, a company active in the medical aesthetics industry. This professional interest is disclosed in order to distinguish his commercial role from the editorial purpose of Beauty Leaders.

A graduate of Amirkabir University of Technology in Biomedical Engineering, he combines technical training with industry experience and a long standing interest in the ethical and human dimensions of aesthetic medicine.
